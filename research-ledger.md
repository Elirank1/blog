## Status definitions

- **PRIMARY VERIFIED** — read directly on the company's own page, press release, or official filing. Highest confidence.
- **SECONDARY CORROBORATED** — not read on a primary source directly (paywalled, inaccessible, or the primary party hasn't published it), but confirmed by multiple independent secondary outlets agreeing on the same specifics.
- **REPORTED** — a single secondary source states it; the primary party involved has not confirmed it, and it isn't corroborated elsewhere. Treated in the essay as "reported," never as settled fact.
- **ESTIMATE** — a named person's or analyst's own figure or projection, explicitly not a disclosed company number. Always attributed by name in the essay, never presented as the company's figure.
- **AUTHOR ANALYSIS** — my own inference, interpretation, or framing choice. Not a claim any cited party has made about themselves. Always flagged in-text with first-person language ("I think," "my reading is," "I'd guess").

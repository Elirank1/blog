## Not independently verified / explicitly flagged as unverifiable in the essay
- Full text of the FT Oct 23 story (paywalled — WebFetch returned SITE_BLOCKED). Essay cites it as "reported," not confirmed.
- Full text of Guillermo Flor's Oct 28 piece beyond the visible excerpt (Substack paywall). His $1.5B figure and the Accenture-shares detail are attributed to him by name, not stated as fact.
- No company in this essay (Anthropic, OpenAI, Ode, Wonderful, Sierra, ServiceNow) has published engagement-level economics (revenue per deployment, margin, expert-hours cost). Section 5 of the essay is explicit that the "software-compounding" curve is hypothetical, not demonstrated.

- That AI deployment has a proven universal margin structure.
- Motives of Anthropic, OpenAI, PE firms beyond what's documented — flagged in §3 as inference, not fact.
- Any Corpiler customer outcome, cross-client productivity gain, or proof of the essay's own thesis.
